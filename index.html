Who we are

Built remote, on purpose

Adaptare comes from the Latin for "to fit". We are designers from across India who wanted to make better products without the commute, the hot desks or the handoff gaps.

We started in 2021 with three designers and a shared Figma file. Today we are a team spread across Pune, Jaipur, Kochi, Bengaluru, Guwahati and a few other places. We meet your timezone half way and write everything down so nothing gets lost.

We turn down projects we can't improve. It keeps our work honest and our clients happy.
